# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, appearance, background, experience, or identity.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and constructive.
- Accepting feedback and differing viewpoints gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project.
- Showing empathy toward other contributors.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, insults, discrimination, personal attacks, publishing private information, or other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional community.

## Responsibility and enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issue content, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to a project maintainer through the contact method listed on the repository profile. Maintainers should respond fairly and promptly.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
